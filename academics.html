Academic Framework & Curriculum

An integrated curriculum harmonizing Cambridge International benchmarks and national standards for all developmental stages.

Intellectual Rigor

A Pathway From Wonder to Mastery

At St. Augustine International Academy, we recognize that learning is an organic, developmental continuum. From the tactile wonders of early childhood to the advanced analytical demands of university prep, our curriculum nurtures critical thinking, conceptual comprehension, and practical application.

Our student-centered pedagogy guarantees individualized attention with small class cohorts (maximum 18 students per class), tech-integrated smart classrooms, and personalized academic coaching.

Early Years Primary School Secondary School Clubs & Sports
Ages 18 Months – 5 Years

1. Early Years & Nursery School (Crèche, Playgroup & KG)

Our Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) fuses British early learning milestones with Montessori experiential learning. We cultivate social confidence, emotional security, fine and gross motor mastery, and foundational language fluency in a secure, loving environment.

Core Learning Areas in Early Years:

  • Synthetic Phonics & Early Reading (Jolly Phonics)
  • Foundational Numeracy & Spatial Awareness
  • Expressive Arts, Music & Sensory Exploration
  • Physical Health, Coordination & Outdoor Play
Ages 6 – 11 Years (Grades 1 – 6)

2. Primary School (Cambridge Primary & National Curriculum)

In Primary School, students build disciplined intellectual habits and conceptual clarity across mathematics, science, digital technology, and humanities. We employ Singapore Mathematics methodologies for deep problem-solving skills alongside inquiry-based science investigations.

Core Primary Subjects:

  • English Language, Literature & Creative Writing
  • Singapore Mathematics & Mental Problem Solving
  • General Science & Experimental Lab Discovery
  • Computer Science, Coding & Digital Citizenship
  • French as a Second International Language
  • Social Studies, Civic Values & Global Geography
Ages 11 – 18 Years (Years 7 – 12 / JSS1 – SSS3)

3. Secondary & High School (Checkpoint, IGCSE & WASSCE)

Our secondary division provides rigorous preparation for elite universities worldwide. We offer comprehensive coaching across three major academic paths: Sciences & Technology, Arts & Humanities, and Business & Commercial Studies.

Junior Secondary (Years 7-9)

Foundational Checkpoint

Broad-based curriculum covering 12 subjects, culminating in the Cambridge Lower Secondary Checkpoint and National BECE.

Senior Secondary (Years 10-12)

IGCSE & WASSCE Tracks

Specialized study paths in STEM, Business, or Liberal Arts with dual certification from Cambridge and West African Examinations Council.

Departmental Subject Breakdown:

🔬 STEM & Sciences
  • Pure Mathematics & Further Maths
  • Physics & Applied Mechanics
  • Chemistry & Chemical Analysis
  • Biology & Environmental Science
  • Technical Drawing & Design
💼 Business & Commerce
  • Economics & Global Trade
  • Financial Accounting & Bookkeeping
  • Business Studies & Entrepreneurship
  • Commerce & Marketing
  • Information Technology (ICT)
🎭 Arts & Humanities
  • English Literature & Creative Writing
  • World History & African Studies
  • Government, Politics & Civics
  • French & Modern Languages
  • Fine Arts, Music & Theatre Arts
Beyond the Classroom

Co-Curricular Clubs & Athletics

We foster well-rounded character through over 35 active student-led societies, sports teams, and artistic groups.

Robotics & AI Lab

Hands-on coding with Python, LEGO Mindstorms, and VEX robotics competitions solving real-world automation challenges.

Debate & Mock Trial

Sharpening persuasive speech, research rigor, and analytical poise through national Model United Nations tournaments.

Music, Orchestra & Choir

Individual instruction in violin, piano, brass, traditional African percussion, and performance choral ensembles.

Competitive Sports & Swimming

Olympic-sized pool, football academy, basketball court, athletics track, and martial arts taekwondo dojo.

Chess & Strategic Games

Fostering foresight, tactical patience, and cognitive pattern recognition through weekly rated tournaments.

Junior Press & Journalism

Producing the termly student journal, photojournalism essays, podcast broadcasts, and campus investigative reviews.
