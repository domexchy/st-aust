About St. Augustine Academy

Discover our legacy of academic distinction, dedicated faculty, and enduring commitment to holistic education.

Our Heritage

A Quarter-Century of Educational Excellence

St. Augustine International Academy was established in September 1998 by a cadre of visionary educationalists led by the late Chief Augustine Oladipo. Starting with just 25 eager pupils and three passionate tutors, our institution was founded on a simple yet revolutionary ideal: that every child deserves an education that sharpens both the mind and the moral conscience.

Over the past 28 years, the Academy has blossomed into one of West Africa's leading international institutions, expanding from our initial early years nursery into an expansive 15-acre, tech-enabled campus encompassing world-class Primary and High School divisions.

Today, our alumni are scholars at Oxford, Harvard, Cambridge, and leading universities globally, serving as innovators, civic leaders, physicians, and entrepreneurs who embody our founding motto: Excellence, Wisdom, and Leadership.

Purpose & Guiding Principles

Mission, Vision & Core Values

The foundational anchors that direct every lesson, interaction, and milestone across our community.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of world-class educational excellence in Africa, nurturing dynamic, morally upright leaders who think globally, innovate courageously, and serve compassionately.

Our Mission

To provide a balanced, rigorous, and technologically advanced learning ecosystem that sparks intellectual inquiry, cultivates creative talent, and instills lifelong ethical principles in every learner.

Leadership Message

Educating for an Unwritten Future

"Dear Parents and Friends, when our students step out into the world, they will enter careers that haven't yet been invented, solve problems we cannot yet foresee, and use technologies still in their infancy.

Our responsibility as educators is not simply to teach children what to think, but to cultivate how to think: to question fearlessly, analyze critically, collaborate generously, and act ethically.

At St. Augustine, our classrooms are vibrant incubators of joy and inquiry. We celebrate mistakes as stepping stones to mastery, and we instill the resilience required to overcome any setback. I warmly invite you to explore our campus and witness this magic firsthand."

Dr. Evelyn Adeleke, B.Sc, M.Ed, Ph.D (Oxon)
Head of School, St. Augustine International Academy
