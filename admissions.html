Admissions & Enrollment

We welcome inquisitive, motivated students into our diverse learning community. Here is everything you need to join our family.

How to Join

The 4-Step Admission Process

Our streamlined admission journey ensures a smooth and supportive transition for every child and family.

1

Submit Application

Obtain and complete the official Admission Application form online or directly from the Admissions Office at our main campus.

2

Assessment & Interview

Applicants undertake an age-appropriate cognitive and literacy assessment, followed by a friendly interactive family dialogue with our academic panel.

3

Offer & Acceptance

Successful applicants receive an official Letter of Admission. Acceptance is secured by returning the acceptance slip and paying the commitment deposit.

4

Orientation & Induction

Families attend orientation day, collect custom uniforms and bookpacks, meet classroom tutors, and ease comfortably into school life.

Checklist

Required Documents for Registration

Please prepare the following certified documents when finalizing your application. Incomplete documentation may delay entrance assessment scheduling.

  • Certified Birth Certificate: Copy of official government-issued birth registry or international passport data page.
  • Academic Transcripts: Official termly report sheets or academic records for the past two (2) academic years (for Primary and Secondary applicants).
  • Medical & Immunization Records: Completed medical fitness certificate signed by a licensed practitioner, including blood group and immunization history.
  • Passport Photographs: Four (4) recent identical passport-sized color photos of the applicant, plus one (1) each for parents/guardians.
  • Transfer Certificate: Signed letter of good conduct or transfer clearance from the child's previous institution.

Sibling Discounts & Merit Scholarships

A 10% tuition discount is granted to the third and subsequent children of the same immediate family enrolled concurrently.

Partial and full Merit Scholarships are awarded annually to exceptional candidates entering Year 7 and Year 10 demonstrating excellence in academics, mathematics, robotics, or musical virtuosity.
