Welcome Note

A Warm Welcome from our Principal

"At St. Augustine Academy, we believe education goes beyond textbooks. Every child has unique potential waiting to be ignited."

Founded on principles of scholarship, character, and service, St. Augustine International Academy offers an inspiring, safe, and academically rigorous haven where young minds grow into confident innovators and compassionate citizens.

Whether your child is taking their first steps in our Nursery or preparing for university matriculation in our High School, our dedicated faculty partners with parents to foster lifelong curiosity and resilience.
